- **Palette:** canvas `F4EEDF`; surface `FFFDFC`; primary text `3D2C28`; secondary text `6F5B53`; primary accent `B85042`; secondary accent `A7BEAE`.
- **Background reference:** Cover uses a warm material canvas or terracotta field; content uses archival evidence only when reliable; section may use one archival cue; closing repeats the material treatment.
- **SVG reference:** `terracotta-stamp` — Optional stamp or archival motif on a historical, evidence, or section page. This is optional guidance, never an automatic decoration quota.
